Soundscapes: Listening to the Environment through Responsible Computing

An interdisciplinary course on environmental acoustics, soundscape ecology, autonomous recording devices, AI-based analysis and responsible environmental computing.

Course overview

Record, analyse and interpret environmental sound responsibly

Each chapter is an independent 60-minute learning unit: 15 minutes of reading, 30 minutes of practice or case study, and 15 minutes of reflection and knowledge check.

How to use this course

  1. Choose a chapter below.
  2. Complete the guided reading.
  3. Carry out the practical activity or case study.
  4. Respond to the reflection prompt and take the knowledge check.

Practical work can use supplied recordings, sample CSVs, Python notebooks or the soundscape device. If equipment is unavailable, use instructor-provided case-study materials.

Course project

Design or evaluate an AI-assisted soundscape monitoring workflow. Plan recording, examine hardware, process environmental audio, apply PANNs and BirdNET, interpret acoustic and semantic indicators, visualise patterns, and evaluate social, ecological and computational implications.

Modular website structure

Each chapter is a separate HTML file. All pages share one stylesheet, so the visual design remains consistent. To add or remove a chapter, update the chapter file and the course directory/navigation links; no redesign of the other chapters is required.
